## Selected work

| --- | --- |
| [GridShift](https://github.com/virajchoudhary/GridShift) | Team carbon-intensity forecasting and GPU-scheduling study. Frozen 2025 SARIMA evaluation reduced mean endpoint MAE by **16.03%** versus persistence. The separate calendar-mean scheduler reduced **modeled** operational CO2 by **12.41%** across 2,913 isolated job cases. [Results and limitations](https://github.com/virajchoudhary/GridShift/blob/main/RESULTS_SUMMARY.md). |
